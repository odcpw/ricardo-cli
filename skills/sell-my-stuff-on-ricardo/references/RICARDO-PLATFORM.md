# Ricardo (and Tutti) platform facts for private sellers

Research snapshot, observed 2026-10-07. German terms are kept where the UI uses them.

## How to use this file

- These are DATED OBSERVATIONS, not current policy. Ricardo edited most cited help pages in the days before this snapshot (updated_at values 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-07), and new AGB and a new invoicing system took effect 2026-10-01.
- Re-verify fees, caps, discounts and deadlines on the live Ricardo pages (listing form shows the exact commission before publishing; "Mein Verkaufen > Gebühren" shows actual fees) before quoting any net figure to the seller.
- NEVER compute a final net payout from the numbers here alone. Use them only to know which cost lines exist and roughly how big they are.
- Anything marked "NOT VERIFIED" is a gap: ask the seller to read it off the live form, or leave it out.
- Method caveat: `help.ricardo.ch` HTML pages return HTTP 403 to the fetch tool. Article bodies were read through Zendesk's public JSON endpoint (`https://help.ricardo.ch/api/v2/help_center/de/articles/<id>.json`, same for `https://www.tutti.help/api/v2/...`) and passed through a summarising fetch tool, so wording is sometimes translated or paraphrased. Quoted German strings are as relayed by that tool; numbers were cross-checked across two or more articles where possible. The human-readable URL for an article is `https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/<id>`.
- Every bullet ends with `(source: URL; observed 2026-10-07)`. "Secondary" marks news or third-party sources.
- Where the "Live observations" section below conflicts with a help-page or AGB bullet, the live observation is the newer and more direct evidence; still re-check it at action time.

## Live observations (signed-in Ricardo interface, 2026-10-07)

Read directly from the signed-in web listing form (`/de/list/`, nothing entered or saved) and from the seller's own historical order records. These supersede the relayed help-page/AGB statements they contradict. Structure is recorded in `docs/seller-routes.md` in the ricardo-cli repository.

- Listing form limits: title max 60 characters; description max 5000; optional "Referenz-Nr." max 40 (use it for the workspace listing/unit ID). (source: Ricardo listing form; observed 2026-10-07)
- Condition labels (exact German UI): "Gebraucht", "Neu und originalverpackt", "Neu (gemäss Beschreibung)", "Defekt". Helper text for Gebraucht: used but functional. (source: Ricardo listing form; observed 2026-10-07)
- Photo slots offered: Hauptbild, three "Jede Seite" (each side), "Mängel" (defects), "Details"; QR-code upload from a phone. Product type ("Produkttyp") becomes selectable after a photo or title is entered; AI title/description helpers exist (review anything they produce). (source: Ricardo listing form; observed 2026-10-07)
- Angebot & Preis: auction toggle with Startpreis and Erhöhungsschritt (default CHF 1.00), optional Sofort-Kaufpreis, Laufzeit with Start and Ende, and the checkbox text "Automatische Reaktivierung deines Angebots bis zu 9 Mal". This conflicts with the relayed AGB "up to 3 times" in section 1; read the current form and AGB at listing time and record which applied. (source: Ricardo listing form; observed 2026-10-07)
- Lieferung: Versand and/or Abholung (pickup address = address stored in the account), seller-set Versandkosten, costs borne by the buyer; Zahlung shows "Barzahlung bei Übergabe" for pickup. Publishing states acceptance of the AGB and that an Erfolgsprovision applies on sale. Buttons: "Entwurf speichern", "Veröffentlichen". (source: Ricardo listing form; observed 2026-10-07)
- Actual commission evidence: Ricardo's order records carry the charged success fee and its rate per order (`ricardo selling sold`: `seller_success_fee_chf`, `seller_success_fee_rate`). Two historical orders (2025) showed rates of 10% and 11%. On the order with buyer-paid shipping, the fee equalled rate × item price **excluding** shipping; on the other, rate × price rounded up to the next CHF 0.05. This contradicts the relayed AGB 5.7 "inklusive Versandkosten" for those orders — the base may have changed with the 2026-10-01 AGB, so verify on the first new sale and use the per-order record for reconciliation. (source: seller order records via Ricardo interface; observed 2026-10-07)
- Seller account signals available read-only: selling limit (current/limit), "orders pending action" flag, unread message count (`ricardo selling status`). The default listing cap observed on the selling page was 100 (`maxAllowedOffers`), consistent with section 1. (source: Ricardo My Selling interface; observed 2026-10-07)

## 1. Listing formats

- Three formats: Auktion, Fixpreis-Angebot ("Sofort kaufen"), and Auktion mit Sofort-Kaufen-Preis. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- Auction: seller sets Angebotsdauer, Startpreis and Erhöhungsschritt. Bidders place single bids or a personal Preislimit (max bid, auto-bids up). Highest bidder at the end gets the Zuschlag. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- After publishing, an Auktion cannot be converted to Fixpreis, nor Fixpreis to Auktion. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- Auction + Sofort-Kaufen: buy-now price must be higher than the start price; a bid equal to the buy-now price is not possible, the buyer must click "Sofort Kaufen", which ends the auction. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- Fixpreis suits mainly new goods, per Ricardo; auctions get more visitors on average and Ricardo suggests ending on a weekend evening. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo's pricing advice: if unsure of value use an auction and set your personal minimum price as the start price; lower start prices raise sell chances; "auctions from CHF 1" often reach higher final prices than comparable higher-start auctions (no guarantee); start in the evening 17:00-21:00 and run 7 days. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/20585436756892; observed 2026-10-07)
- Soft close: a bid in the last 3 minutes extends the auction to 3 minutes after that bid, repeatedly. Same rule in the AGB. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000380769; observed 2026-10-07)
- Minimum price / reserve: no separate hidden reserve is documented. The "Mindestpreis" is the Startpreis (AGB wording: bidding begins "beim festgelegten Mindestpreis"). NOT VERIFIED that the form has no reserve field. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/20585436756892 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Prices are CHF only; smallest payable unit 5 Rappen. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002833405; observed 2026-10-07)
- Preisvorschlag (price offers): fixed-price listings only, enabled in the sales form under "Listing Type & Price" via "Allow price proposals" (English as relayed; German UI labels not captured); not available for auctions. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017004739; observed 2026-10-07)
- Preisvorschlag rules: proposal >= 70% of your price; seller has 48 h to accept or reject, silence = expiry; accepting makes the sale binding; one pending proposal per buyer, up to 3 per buyer in total; shipping is not part of the proposed price; other open proposals cancel when one is accepted. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017004739; observed 2026-10-07)
- Unsold ended listings accept price proposals from buyers for 60 days after the end; the option is on by default for them. Open proposals are auto-rejected when a listing is reactivated. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017004739 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4402999257490; observed 2026-10-07)
- A price proposal is a binding purchase offer (AGB 6.2.4). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Quantity listings (Mehrfachangebote): 2 to 99 units, fixed price only (no auctions), price applies per unit, shipping is paid once when a buyer takes several units unless the description says otherwise. A set at one total price must use quantity 1. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002517525; observed 2026-10-07)
- Creation routes: web form (drafts, custom shipping costs), app form (photo first), Ricardo AI price estimate in the app (title and description drafted by AI; review before publishing), copy offer ("Sell similar" on a product page also works on other users' offers), copy draft, bulk tool "Mehrere Artikel" (web only, up to 100 offers at once), product feed (commercial), and republishing from tutti.ch or anibis.ch. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002488565; observed 2026-10-07)
- Drafts: unlimited, kept until published. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360020022720; observed 2026-10-07)
- Active plus planned offers per account are capped at 100 by default; higher limits on request for mostly second-hand/collectible stock with >= 10% monthly sell-through. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000369620 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003892009; observed 2026-10-07)
- Scheduled start: listings can be "geplant" with a later start (boost then starts at listing start; commission rules mention scheduled listings). How far ahead you can schedule: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21308881293084 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000171809; observed 2026-10-07)
- Duration options (days allowed for auctions / fixed price): NOT VERIFIED. Help pages only say the seller defines the duration. A web-search snippet attributed to Ricardo help said fixed-price listings can run "bis zu 4 Wochen"; the article body I fetched contained no such sentence. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360002175139; observed 2026-10-07)
- Manual relist: "Mein Verkaufen > Am Verkaufen > Erneut verkaufen" (single or "Mehrere auswählen"). Unchanged relist keeps the offer on watchlists; any edit creates a new item number and drops watchlists. A price cut of 10% or more triggers an e-mail to watchers. Unsold listings are deleted after 365 days. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4402999257490 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360011827520; observed 2026-10-07)
- Automatic reactivation (AGB 5.5.1, text valid from 2026-10-01): an auction "kann bis zu 3 Mal automatisch reaktiviert werden" (as relayed; condition in AGB summary: only if no bid); a fixed-price listing cannot be auto-reactivated, except quantity listings (>1) where at least one unit sold. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Relist fees: creating/relisting has no listing fee (vehicles excepted); chosen boosts are charged again on each reactivation, including automatic ones; the vehicle listing fee is not charged again on reactivation. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21308881293084 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21356107763740; observed 2026-10-07)
- Every offer gets one of four conditions, which buyers can filter on: "New and original packaging", "New (as described)", "Used", "Defective" (English as relayed; exact German UI labels NOT VERIFIED, probably Neu/originalverpackt, Neu, Gebraucht, Defekt). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002488945; observed 2026-10-07)

## 2. Fees for private sellers

Re-verify every number in this section live. Ricardo changed its fee model several times and edited these pages within days of this snapshot.

- No fee distinction between private and commercial sellers: "Alle Verkäufer haben dieselbe Erfolgsprovision". (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000370999; observed 2026-10-07)
- Insertion fee (Einstellgebühr): none for normal listings. Exception: vehicle listings priced from CHF 3,000, refundable (see below). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002854885; observed 2026-10-07)
- Vehicle listing fee (since 2026-01-12): Auto, Motorrad, Nutzfahrzeug, Camping-Fahrzeug. Price under 3,000 free; 3,000-9,999.95 = CHF 24.90; 10,000-19,999.95 = 34.90; 20,000-39,999.95 = 45.90; from 40,000 = 55.90. One-time at first publication, refunded in full on a sale completed on Ricardo, charged again if the sale is cancelled, not charged on reactivation. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21356107763740; observed 2026-10-07)
- Success commission (Erfolgsprovision): 8-12% of the sale price depending on Produkttyp, minimum CHF 0.10, maximum CHF 290 per sale. The exact amount is shown in the listing form before publishing. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000171809; observed 2026-10-07)
- The rate is set at product-type level (category > area > product type > product). Declaring a wrong product type to get a lower commission is prohibited. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000171809; observed 2026-10-07)
- Sample rates: 8% pet supplies; 9% books, comics, audiobooks; 10% smartphones and accessories, art and collectibles (stamps, coins, trading cards, albums); 11% vehicles and parts, watches/clocks; 12% PC and gaming, furniture/house/garden, fashion, leisure (cameras, instruments, toys, bicycles, skis, tickets). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000171809; observed 2026-10-07)
- Full per-product-type table exists. Do not guess a rate from a category name; read the form. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/8238144523036; observed 2026-10-07)
- Fee base: AGB 5.7 reportedly says the base is the achieved sale price "inklusive Versandkosten", before deducting the commission, at the tax rate valid at sale time. This came from two summariser passes over the AGB; confirm in the AGB text or by comparing a real sale's fee. Ricardo's own help pages say only "% des Verkaufspreises". (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Commission discounts since 2026-04-01, each 10% off the commission amount, stackable up to 100%, computed before the CHF 0.10 / CHF 290 limits, only on completed sales, never retroactive: (a) auction with start price CHF 1 or less (also if it has buy-now, or if a price proposal is accepted after an unsuccessful auction); (b) Ricardo Plus active and buyer paid with Käuferschutz+; (c) every 5th sale counted from 2026-04-01 (not if that 5th sale is cancelled); (d) high-volume sellers with net revenue >= CHF 100,000 in 12 months (checked 15 March and 15 September; (c) and (d) cannot combine). Temporary promo discounts may also apply. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/26398881565084; observed 2026-10-07)
- Context for the discounts (secondary): Ricardo agreed with the Swiss Price Supervisor to implement 10% discounts on success fees by 1 April 2026 at the latest. (source: https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2026-01-07/preisueberwacher-regelt-tarife-grosser-anzeigenplattformen; observed 2026-10-07)
- Fee history (secondary, search-engine snippets of itreseller.ch/Blick/SRF, not fetched): 2022 flat 10% with CHF 290 cap, 8-12% by product type since 2023. Useful only to explain why older blogs quote other numbers. (source: https://www.itreseller.ch/Artikel/96779/Ricardo_erhoeht_die_Gebuehren_auf_10_Prozent.html; observed 2026-10-07)
- Do not trust blog numbers like "5-8%" (zunapro.com guide, secondary): they contradict the official 8-12%. (source: https://www.zunapro.com/switzerland/de/blog/ricardo-ch-marketplace-verkaeufer-guide; observed 2026-10-07)
- Paid add-ons are called Sichtbarkeits-Boosts: Bronze, Silber, Gold. Price depends on product type and is shown only in the sales form; Ricardo publishes no price table. Prices: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21308881293084; observed 2026-10-07)
- Boost placements: first search position, first among similar offers, personalised homepage, personal e-mails to buyers. Only one boosted offer is shown per slot at a time, picked at random among relevant boosted offers. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000171969; observed 2026-10-07)
- Boost billing: due when the listing is published; applies to one listing run; charged again on reactivation (also automatic); no refund if the item sells early or you end it; payable even if unsold; upgrade within 24 h possible, ask support for the price-difference refund; quantity listings are charged once. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/21308881293084; observed 2026-10-07)
- Old-style add-ons named in the task (bold, gallery, Top-Platzierung) are not part of today's help pages; only boosts were found. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002854885; observed 2026-10-07)
- Käuferschutz+ costs the seller nothing; the buyer pays a service fee (varies with item price excluding shipping, minimum CHF 0.50, non-refundable). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11706179687452; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo Plus is free (no subscription). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/24879694213020; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo shipping labels are billed to the seller's Ricardo account when the parcel is scanned by the Post (see section 4). A reminder fee of CHF 10 applies to overdue invoices. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360016062700 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/30638525823516; observed 2026-10-07)
- Billing (new system since 2026-10-01): fees, credits and refunds are bundled into 30-day periods; on day 31 an invoice is issued; a negative balance becomes a credit note paid to the stored IBAN (only if no open invoices). Pay by TWINT/card in the account or bank transfer. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/30638528183708 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/30638615526940; observed 2026-10-07)
- Invoice deadlines, private accounts: pay within 20 days; reminder at day 21; account blocked and CHF 10 fee at day 31; collection partner at day 41. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/30638525823516; observed 2026-10-07)
- Käuferschutz+ sales are different: Käuferschutz+ terms say commission and other fees are deducted from the purchase price before payout to the seller (clause 4.1). Whether this changes with the new invoicing system: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/10559969349916; observed 2026-10-07)
- VAT: Ricardo collects VAT on sales to CH/LI buyers since 2025-01-01. Private sellers "need not do anything"; a VAT number is required only when business revenue exceeds CHF 100,000. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/16882997491996; observed 2026-10-07)
- Temporary promotions exist, e.g. Gratisversand campaign 2-8 Dec 2025 (Ricardo paid B-Post shipping for CH items >= CHF 25, quantity 1, shown only to some user groups). No standing free-listing promotion applies because listing is already free. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/20171266035484; observed 2026-10-07)

Cost lines a net calculation must still pull from the live form or fee page: commission (rate by product type, discounts, CHF 0.10 floor, CHF 290 cap, base incl. or excl. shipping), boost, vehicle fee, Ricardo label cost if used, whether commission is deducted from payout or invoiced.

## 3. Payment, payout, protection

Two regimes exist. Check which one the listing is in.

### 3a. Käuferschutz+ (integrated payment, via Adyen)

- Buyer pays by TWINT or debit/credit card; bank transfer is not available for protected purchases. Ricardo holds the money until the buyer confirms receipt. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11705673253916 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11706288346780; observed 2026-10-07)
- Conditions: auction or fixed price, price under CHF 5,000, package shipping selected, Swiss addresses for both sides (buyer address recognised by the Post), seller on a private account with a valid Swiss IBAN in the seller's own name (pooled accounts such as Revolut excluded), Ricardo Plus active. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11706288346780; observed 2026-10-07)
- Seller steps: buyer pays; Ricardo notifies you; ship within 7 days after payment confirmation (terms: 7 Kalendertage) with the Ricardo label or your own label (then mark shipped in "Mein Verkaufen"). Shipping late cancels the sale and refunds the buyer. If neither shipment nor receipt is confirmed within 10 days of payment the sale is cancelled. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11782946212764 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11706288346780; observed 2026-10-07)
- Buyer check window: with Ricardo label, 3 days after delivery to confirm or report; silence = auto-complete and payout. With your own label, buyer has 6 days to confirm receipt, then 3 days to report problems. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11782946212764; observed 2026-10-07)
- Payout: after the 72 h inspection period without complaint Ricardo instructs Adyen to pay the seller's bank account. Time from instruction to bank credit: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/10559969349916; observed 2026-10-07)
- Express Auszahlung (automatic, rolling out): >= 100 completed sales per year, rating >= 99.0%, account >= 12 months, 2FA on. Payout is triggered right after the buyer pays and arrives in 2-4 business days, before shipping. Switched off when 2FA is disabled or a complaint is decided for the buyer. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/19958918456476; observed 2026-10-07)
- Adyen may temporarily block payouts for compliance checks (ID or bank statement upload may be requested). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/19958918456476; observed 2026-10-07)
- Buyer problem cases: not delivered (claim window 10-30 days after payment), wrong item, clearly not as described, counterfeit, damaged/defective. Ricardo mediates up to two weeks, then decides the payout split; approved returns use a Ricardo return label (buyer has 7 days, seller 72 h to acknowledge). If decided for the buyer: sale cancelled, sales fees credited to seller, buyer refunded price and shipping. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11708179472412, https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/10559969349916 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/19958918456476; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo Plus: only for private sellers resident in Switzerland (no PO boxes, Swiss IBAN needed); activating it switches Käuferschutz+ on for all current and future qualifying listings; it can also be auto-enabled if Käuferschutz+ was already active; deactivating removes protection from active listings except auctions that already have bids and finished sales. Includes Ferienmodus (max 21 days, sets a pause on shipping duty). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/24879063770140, https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/24879694213020 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/24878574477852; observed 2026-10-07)
- Whether Ricardo Plus / Käuferschutz+ is a hard prerequisite to sell: the "Wie werde ich Verkäufer" checklist lists enabling Käuferschutz+ for private individuals as step 6, but "Wieso kann ich nicht verkaufen?" does not list it as a blocker. NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003832545 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003892009; observed 2026-10-07)

### 3b. Direct payment (no Käuferschutz+)

- The seller defines payment methods in the listing. Buyers see: bank transfer / e-banking as Vorauszahlung, or cash on pickup (Barzahlung bei Abholung). No PayPal or TWINT in this path in Ricardo's own text. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000386349; observed 2026-10-07)
- After a sale Ricardo e-mails the buyer your payment details if your IBAN/bank details are stored under "Zahlungen"; otherwise you must contact the buyer. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002846569; observed 2026-10-07)
- Deadlines (AGB and help): both sides have 7 days after the end to make contact (not needed for prepayment if your IBAN is stored); buyer pays within 14 days of getting payment details; seller ships within 14 days after payment (extra declared availability can be added); pickup should happen within 14 days. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000396525 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000396485; observed 2026-10-07)
- A concluded auction or buy-now is a legally binding contract. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002695009; observed 2026-10-07)
- Seller protection (Verkäuferschutz): up to CHF 250 per claim, deductible CHF 10 or 10% of price (max CHF 25); claim 30-60 days after the end; covers delivered-but-unpaid and parcel returned unclaimed (deductible CHF 5). Excludes transport loss, normal letter post, "Barzahlung; Abholung" listings, event tickets. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002931925; observed 2026-10-07)
- Buyer-side Käuferschutz for direct payments: up to CHF 250 per claim, min purchase CHF 10, claim 30-60 days after end. Not available if Käuferschutz+ was available but not chosen. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4418013246482; observed 2026-10-07)

### 3c. Buyer does not pay

- If the buyer does not respond after 7 days or has not paid after 14 days: contact twice by e-mail and once by phone. Then either cancel and get the commission credited, or insist (reminder e-mail, "Mahn-E-Mail im Namen von Ricardo" form; legal action is yours, Ricardo does not sue). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002695009 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/18447206092572; observed 2026-10-07)
- Cancel sale ("Verkauf stornieren") is possible 7 to 60 days after the offer ends. Ricardo asks the buyer; if the buyer agrees or is silent for 7 days it auto-approves and the commission is credited. The at-fault party usually gets a negative rating. The fees article says already-billed fees must still be paid within 30 days and the credit is netted against later fees (wording may predate the 2026-10-01 invoicing system, which sets a 20-day term for private accounts). If the buyer pays after you cancelled, ask support to withdraw the request. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002848429 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000396525; observed 2026-10-07)
- A foreign buyer buying despite "no shipping abroad" in the text: sale void; cancellation request possible 7 days after the end. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003690725; observed 2026-10-07)

## 4. Shipping and pickup

- The seller defines shipping options and costs per listing; the buyer pays the stated costs. Options: shipping, pickup, or both. Large heavy items: Ricardo suggests pickup only. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/20585482632476 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/20585436756892; observed 2026-10-07)
- Shipping option codes in Ricardo's product feed (likely mirrors the form; inference): pickup by buyer, Briefpost A/B (B5 100 g or 250 g, 2 cm or 5 cm thick; B4 500 g or 1000 g, 2 cm), Einschreiben, Paketpost A/B (2, 10, 30 kg), Sperrgut Priority/Economy (30 kg), Kurierdienst, Lieferung durch Verkäufer, Transportunternehmen, Versand aus dem Ausland. ## 5. Seller obligations and policies

- Description duty (AGB 5.3): truthful, not misleading statements about the item. Statements that should not count as warranted assurances must be clearly marked. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Warranty: Ricardo's help pages say nothing like "gekauft wie gesehen" for private sellers. AGB 6.2.6c (as relayed): the seller is liable for Sachmängel, i.e. undescribed defects that significantly impair value or use. Practical rule: list every defect; whether an explicit "Gewährleistung ausgeschlossen" line is effective on Ricardo: NOT VERIFIED (Ricardo links a "Rechtliche Fragen & Antworten" Google Drive PDF that was not read). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002934305; observed 2026-10-07)
- Description, photos and public Q&A together count as part of the purchase contract. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003669565; observed 2026-10-07)
- Contract formation: when the listing period ends a binding contract exists with the highest bidder; once any bid exists the seller is bound (AGB 6.2.1, 6.2.5). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417210291858; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ending/editing: with no bids you can edit or end in "Mein Verkaufen > Am Verkaufen". With a bid you can only add text and images; you cannot end or delete the auction. If you cannot sell, add a note to the description and contact the buyer after the end. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003698429; observed 2026-10-07)
- Removing an accidental bid is possible only while the auction runs and only with seller consent via customer service; this deletes all of that bidder's bids. After the end, cancel the sale (not before day 7). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017227399; observed 2026-10-07)
- Photos: 1 to 10 images per listing (cross-posting article and Q&A rule on "additional images if the listing already has 10"); Ricardo crops to 4:3, so horizontal photos look best. Photo file size limit: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://www.tutti.help/hc/de/articles/22901414630674, https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002488945; observed 2026-10-07)
- Description length: under 5,000 characters (from tutti-to-Ricardo import rules). Title length: NOT VERIFIED. (source: https://www.tutti.help/hc/de/articles/22901414630674; observed 2026-10-07)
- Languages: listing text may be German, French, Italian or English; other languages are removal grounds. Buyers get a machine-translation button (no liability for translation accuracy). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417502093202 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4406410301586; observed 2026-10-07)
- Prohibited (selection from the list updated 2026-09-30): counterfeits and replicas, stolen goods, medicines, ID documents, used motorway vignettes, drugs incl. CBD, weapons and imitations, live animals, real estate, securities/crypto/current banknotes, cigarettes (not cigars/pipe tobacco), active licence plates, personalised tickets with restricted transfer, non-transferable vouchers, contact details in listings, off-platform sale hints, search-manipulating keywords, duplicate listings, wrong category. Ricardo may ask for proof of ownership. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4417502093202 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002766985; observed 2026-10-07)
- No contact details (phone, e-mail, links, social, bank data) in listing, Q&A or ratings. Sale outside Ricardo is not allowed. IBANs, pickup details and tracking numbers may be shared in Ricardo's post-sale "Nachrichten"; messages and contact stay available 60 days after the transaction. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/29424855027868; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ratings (Bewertungen): mutual, positive/neutral/negative with a text; seller rating comment up to 180 characters; rating percentage = positives / (positives + negatives), neutrals ignored; reminder 30 days after the end; no deadline for rating; too many negatives get a member excluded; truthful and factual text only, no advertising or contact data, reply-to-rating comments are deleted. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002896445, https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002893209, https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002896465 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360011827520; observed 2026-10-07)
- Dispute handling outside Käuferschutz+: talk first, then Ricardo's cancellation and protection forms (sections 3b, 3c). Ombudsstelle for second-hand platforms exists for Ricardo, tutti.ch and anibis.ch (title only seen). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4802152851346 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000396525; observed 2026-10-07)
- Fraud signals: contact via Q&A, WhatsApp or e-mail pushing links; do not leave the platform. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409; observed 2026-10-07)

## 6. Questions to the seller (Fragen & Antworten)

- Verified members ask through the "Question to seller" feature on the listing page; the seller gets an e-mail and answers from "Mein Verkaufen > Am Verkaufen" (blue arrow "Details", then the answer button) or via the mail link. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409; observed 2026-10-07)
- Answers are public by default (visible to logged-in members) and may be set private (only asker and seller). One answer per question; corrections only via customer service; Q&A cannot be deleted (legally part of the listing); Q&A survive reactivation. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409; observed 2026-10-07)
- The answer is "binding part of the offer". (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003669565; observed 2026-10-07)
- Automatic moderation hides violating content silently. Allowed: phone numbers only in vehicle categories (to arrange viewings); extra photos if the listing already has 10. Repeated breaches can suspend the account. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002754409; observed 2026-10-07)
- Required answer time: NOT VERIFIED (no deadline found). Fast answers support sales; slow ones lose bids.

## 7. Account requirements to sell

- Residence in Switzerland or Liechtenstein; private individuals living abroad cannot sell. Foreign commercial sellers need a written application, a commercial registration and the activation code. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003832545 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002833405; observed 2026-10-07)
- Steps: free account, e-mail confirmation, phone number confirmed by SMS code, then verification by either online ID check (document upload, partner Onfido, may ask a face photo) or an activation code sent by A-Post to a CH/LI address. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003832545 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4406048432274; observed 2026-10-07)
- For private sellers: enable Käuferschutz+ (see 3a note on whether mandatory). Bank details/IBAN are stored under "Zahlungen"; for Käuferschutz+/Ricardo Plus the IBAN must be Swiss and in the account holder's name. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003832545 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/11765320870428; observed 2026-10-07)
- Limits for new accounts beyond the 100-offer default: NOT VERIFIED (no separate new-account limit found). (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360000369620; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo mentions DAC7 reporting for EU platform sellers; not relevant to CH private sellers. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002833405; observed 2026-10-07)
- Ricardo belongs to Swiss Marketplace Group (SMG) with tutti.ch and anibis.ch; the privacy policy is SMG's. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/4802152851346; observed 2026-10-07)

## 9. Where Ricardo differs from eBay (for porting an eBay workflow)

eBay-side statements below are general knowledge, not researched today; Ricardo-side statements cite sources.

- Fee model: one commission (8-12% by product type, CHF 0.10-290 cap) and no insertion fee (vehicles >= CHF 3,000 excepted); no eBay-style per-order fixed fee or store tiers found. Same rates for private and commercial. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002854885; observed 2026-10-07)
- No eBay Store equivalent was found for private sellers; the only "shop-like" tooling found is the product feed for commercial sellers and a 100-offer cap. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002488565; observed 2026-10-07)
- Commission discount for auctions starting at CHF 1 or less rewards the Swiss "1-Franken-Auktion" culture; low start price is the recommended mechanic. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/26398881565084; observed 2026-10-07)
- CHF only, 5 Rappen steps, domestic shipping assumed, no CH-only switch, private foreign sellers excluded. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115002833405 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003690725; observed 2026-10-07)
- Bids are binding for the bidder and also bind the seller: once any bid exists the seller cannot end or delete the auction. A bid can be removed only while the auction runs, with the seller's consent via customer service. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003698429 and https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017227399; observed 2026-10-07)
- Payment is Ricardo-specific: Käuferschutz+ via Adyen (TWINT/card), otherwise bank transfer or cash on pickup. Not PayPal. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115000386349; observed 2026-10-07)
- Q&A is public and part of the contract; contact details anywhere are prohibited. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/115003669565; observed 2026-10-07)
- Buy-now plus auction exists; Preisvorschlag (best-offer analogue) only on fixed-price listings with a 70% floor and 48 h response. (source: https://help.ricardo.ch/hc/de/articles/360017004739; observed 2026-10-07)
